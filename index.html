Transport Costs by Mode

India moves 66% of its freight by road at ₹3.78 per tonne-km. Rail carries 31% at ₹1.96 per tonne-km - nearly half the road cost. Coastal shipping at ₹1.80 per tonne-km is the cheapest surface mode, yet handles just 2% of freight. Air freight at ₹72 per tonne-km serves the remaining 1%.

The dominance of road freight - despite being the most expensive surface mode - signals a massive optimization opportunity. AI-driven modal selection can identify when rail or coastal options offer better economics, automatically routing shipments to the lowest-cost mode. The break-even point for road-to-multimodal shifts occurs at approximately 600 km.
